Courses :

BCA :

The Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA) is a 3-year undergraduate degree program focused on computer science, programming, and software development. Course Overview & Eligibility
• Duration: 3 years, divided into 6 semesters (some institutions offer a 4-year honors option).
• Eligibility: Passing class 12th from a recognized board with a minimum of 45% to 50% aggregate marks. Some colleges prefer or require Mathematics or Computer Science/Information Practices in 10+2.
• Admission: Based on merit or entrance exams like CUET, IPU CET, or specific university tests.
Core Subjects
• Programming Languages: C, C++, Java, and Python.
• IT & Computing: Database Management Systems (DBMS), Data Structures, and Operating Systems.
• Web & Software: Web technologies (HTML, CSS, JavaScript) and Software Engineering.
Career Scope & Salary
• Job Roles: Software Developer, Web Developer, Data Analyst, System Administrator, and IT Support.
• Top Recruiters: TCS, Infosys, Wipro, HCL, Cognizant, and Accenture.
• Average Salary: Entry-level salaries generally range from ₹2.5 LPA to ₹5 LPA in India.
• Higher Education: Graduates can pursue an MCA, MBA (IT), or specialized tech certifications.

BBA :

A Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA) is a 3-year undergraduate management degree that prepares students for careers in corporate leadership, business operations, and entrepreneurship. Key Course Details
• Full Form: Bachelor of Business Administration
• Duration: 3 years (divided into 6 semesters); some institutions also offer a 4-year advanced Honors degree.
• Eligibility: Completion of Class 12 (10+2) from any stream (Science, Commerce, or Arts) with a minimum aggregate of 45% to 50%.
• Admission Process: Based on merit or national/university-level entrance exams like CUET, IPMAT, or SUAT, often followed by personal interviews.
• Average Fees: Ranges from ₹20,000 to ₹80,000 per year in government institutions, and ₹80,000 to ₹3,00,000+ per year in private colleges.
Popular Specializations
• Marketing Management
• Financial Management
• Human Resource Management (HRM)
• Business Analytics
• International Business
• Digital Marketing
Career Scope & Salary
• Starting Salary: Generally between ₹3 LPA to ₹7 LPA depending on the college and skill set.
• Top Job Roles: Business Development Executive, Marketing Associate, HR Trainee, and Financial Analyst.
• Higher Education: Many graduates choose to pursue an MBA (Master of Business Administration) or a PGDM for advanced career growth.

BTECH :

A B.Tech (Bachelor of Technology) is a four-year undergraduate engineering degree split into eight semesters that blends scientific theory with practical, hands-on laboratory work and projects. Course Overview
• Duration: 4 years (8 semesters)
• Eligibility: Class 12 (10+2) with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics (PCM) with a minimum of 50% to 60% marks.
• Admission Exams: JEE Main, JEE Advanced, BITSAT, VITEEE, and various state-level entrance tests.
Popular Specializations
• Computer Science and Engineering (CSE): Focuses on coding, algorithms, software development, and databases.
• Artificial Intelligence and Data Science: Centers on machine learning, neural networks, and big data analytics.
• Electronics and Communication (ECE): Deals with digital circuits, embedded systems, and wireless tech.
• Mechanical Engineering: Covers thermodynamics, machine design, and manufacturing processes.
• Civil Engineering: Focuses on structural design, construction methods, and infrastructure development.
Career Scope
Graduates can work as software engineers, data analysts, mechanical designers, or civil consultants, or pursue higher education like an M.Tech or MBA
