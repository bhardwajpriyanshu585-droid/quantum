KIET Group of Institutions, Ghaziabad

KIET Deemed to be University (formerly KIET Group of Institutions) is a premier multidisciplinary institution located on the Delhi-NCR Ghaziabad-Meerut Road, Uttar Pradesh. Overview & History
• Established: 1998 under the aegis of Krishna Charitable Society.
• Status: Conferred Autonomous status in 2024 and upgraded to a Deemed-to-be University status in late 2025 under Section 3 of the UGC Act, 1956. It is no longer affiliated with AKTU/UPTAC for admissions.
• Accreditations: Accredited with NAAC A+ grade and NBA-accredited programs; holds a QS I-GAUGE Diamond rating.
Academics & Schools The university offers undergraduate, postgraduate, diploma, and doctoral programs across four primary schools:
• School of Engineering & Technology: B.Tech, M.Tech
• School of Pharmacy: B.Pharm, D.Pharm, and Ph.D.
• School of Management: MBA and management programs
• School of Computer Applications: MCA and computer applications Admissions
• Flagship B.Tech Admissions: Now conducts its own entrance test called the KIET Entrance Examination (KEE) alongside accepting JEE Main scores and composite merit.
• Application Mode: Online via the Official KIET Website.
